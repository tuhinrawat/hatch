Hatch

Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: Hatch has no accounts and no tracking, and nothing it sends identifies you. Your data stays on your Mac. The app only goes online for artwork, lyrics and update checks, plus a once-a-day count of which version is in use.

What stays on your Mac

  • To-dos, notes and settings are saved on your Mac only.
  • Tray files are copied to a folder on your Mac (~/Library/Application Support/Hatch) and never uploaded.
  • Clipboard history is kept in memory only and is gone when Hatch quits. Items that password managers mark as private are never recorded.

Permissions, and why

  • Calendar: to show today's and tomorrow's events and find meeting links. Events are read on your Mac and never sent anywhere.
  • Automation (Music, Spotify and browsers): to show and control what's playing. In browsers, Hatch reads only the playing video's title, time and artwork.

When Hatch goes online

  • Lyrics: for Music and Spotify, Hatch asks LRCLIB, a free open lyrics database, for the current song, sending its title, artist, album and length. You can turn lyrics off in Settings.
  • Artwork: album art and video thumbnails are fetched from the address the player or website provides.
  • Update check: a few times a day Hatch downloads myhatch.dev/version.json to see whether a new version is out. The request contains nothing about you, only the app version.
  • Daily version count: once a day Hatch tells myhatch.dev its version number (for example "0.9.6"), so we can count how many copies are in use. Nothing else is sent: no ID, name, serial number or device details. We keep only daily totals per version, for 90 days, and don't store IP addresses.

Hatch sends no usage details, crash reports or identifiers.

This website

We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you; it reports totals such as page views, referring sites and countries. Downloads are served by GitHub, which counts how many times each release is downloaded.

Feedback

If you send feedback through GitHub, it's public and handled under GitHub's privacy policy. Only include what you're happy to share.

Questions

Open an issue at myhatch.dev/feedback.
